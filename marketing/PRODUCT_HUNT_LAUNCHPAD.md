```markdown
👋 Hey Product Hunt community!

I'm Miroslav, creator of **Prep-it**.

### **Why we built this:**
Like many software engineers, I dreaded preparing for technical interviews. Every job hunt started the same way: downloading massive GitHub repos with 500+ static questions, trying to read through them on weekends, burning out after 20 minutes, and suffering from illusory competence.

You don't need 3 uninterrupted hours to prepare. You need a daily habit.

We turned tech interview prep into a 10-minute micro-learning habit using the Tinder swipe mechanic:
- 📱 **Active Recall:** Swipe Right if you know it cold, Swipe Left if you're unsure.
- 🧠 **SuperMemo-2 Spaced Repetition:** The algorithm automatically schedules weak questions for review before you forget them.
- 🤖 **Instant AI Breakdowns:** When a concept doesn't click, our OpenRouter AI engine gives you a senior-level explanation with code snippets in 5 seconds.
- ⚡ **Zero-Friction Telegram Mini App & Web:** Runs in 300ms directly inside Telegram or as a desktop PWA without installing anything.
```
